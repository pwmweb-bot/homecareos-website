Carer: record medicines (eMAR)

The eMAR on your phone shows only the medicines due at this visit, plus any as-needed (PRN) medicines.

Live Every agency has this today.

Steps

  1. 1

    Open Medications on the visit

    Tap Medications. Medicines due this visit are listed first, then As needed (PRN).

  2. 2

    Read any warning

    If HomecareOS shows a warning before you give a dose, read it before you go on. Allergies, controlled and high-risk medicines are marked.

  3. 3

    Choose the outcome

    Record what happened, for example Given, Refused, Withheld or Missed. Add a note if it helps.

  4. 4

    For a PRN medicine, give the reason

    Say why you gave it.

  5. 5

    For a patch, mark the site

    Tap the body map to record where the patch was placed.

  6. 6

    Submit

    The dose is recorded against the visit with your name and the time.

Good to know

  • If a second carer needs to witness a dose, the app asks for them.
  • No signal? Doses are kept on your phone and sent when signal returns.
