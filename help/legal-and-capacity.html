Legal and capacity: decisions and signed papers

Safety > Legal and capacity is one screen of cards for the decisions that must be clear in an emergency: CPR, power of attorney, DoLS, an advance decision, an advance statement and the Mental Health Act. Each card shows what is in force now, with its signed paper, and earlier records folded underneath.

Live Every agency has this today.

Before you start

  • Open the person's record, then Safety, then Legal and capacity.
  • Recording or changing these decisions, and filing their papers, needs permission to edit the person's record, and your role must be on the agency's legal records list.

Steps

  1. 1

    Read what is in force now

    The CPR card is the wide one. Each card shows the current decision in large type, or says it is not recorded. Earlier records fold under the card, such as 1 earlier decision, and each opens its own record. Tags such as DNACPR in place appear beside the person's name when that read has worked.

  2. 2

    Record or change a decision

    Each card has its own action. CPR with nothing recorded offers Record the CPR decision. Once there is a decision, use Record a new decision (it supersedes the previous one) or Correct this one, which opens the form already filled in and saves a new version, keeping the old one. For a power of attorney use Add another LPA (in Scotland and Northern Ireland this reads Add another power of attorney). For DoLS use Record the renewal. For an advance decision or a Mental Health Act record use Record one, or Record a new one if one is already there. For an advance statement use Change.

  3. 3

    If two CPR decisions disagree

    The card says Two CPR decisions disagree, never DNACPR. Choose Record which one applies. Saving it retires the other.

  4. 4

    Add a signed paper

    Drag a file onto the card, or choose a file (on a phone the button reads Add a photo or file). A short confirm then shows Filed as, Linked to and Show to carers. Choose Save the document. If nothing is recorded on that card yet, the confirm offers Save, then record its details, which files the paper and opens the form.

  5. 5

    View, replace or show it to carers

    A paper already on the card has View, Replace and Show to carers or Stop showing to carers. Replacing files a new paper and keeps the earlier one. Nothing is deleted. Show to carers is on by default for a ReSPECT or DNACPR form, and off for every other paper.

  6. 6

    Drop a paper in other places too

    The same drop box is on Consent, Advanced care (the end of life plan and the contingency plan), Referrals, Transfers of care, the care plan's ReSPECT upload and Medical history. On a record's own page the drop is labelled Signed paper.

Good to know

  • The power of attorney card is called Lasting power of attorney in England and Wales, Power of attorney in Scotland, and Enduring power of attorney in Northern Ireland.
  • The CPR card also shows treatment escalation as four tiles.
  • A paper is linked to one record only, and a linked paper is never deleted.
