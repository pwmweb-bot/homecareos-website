homecareOS vs ShiftCare

ShiftCare was built for the Australian market and adapted for UK. homecareOS was built from day one for UK regulatory requirements, council billing, and NHS integration.

ShiftCare

ShiftCare is an international platform spanning Australia, the US, and the UK, covering domiciliary and disability care with eMAR, open shift posting, and EVV built in. homecareOS is built UK-first with NHS Assured status, full CQC compliance tooling, and complete council billing support.
