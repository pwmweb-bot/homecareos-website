Getting started: five steps for a new agency

Do these in order and your first carer can clock in to your first visit.

  1. 1 Add your first service user Name, address and how carers get in. The record then shows what is still missing.
  2. 2 Build their care plan What matters to them, the tasks for each visit, medicines and risks.
  3. 3 Schedule their visits Put the visits on the rota and book a carer.
  4. 4 Add your carers and invite them to the app Each carer needs an email address to get their invite.
  5. 5 Carers sign in and clock in Carers download the app, sign in and see their visits.
