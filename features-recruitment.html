Recruitment That Actually Works

Six capabilities that transform how you find, vet and onboard care staff.

Branded Application Portal

A professional multi-step application flow on your own domain. Candidates complete everything before you review.

Applicant Tracking

Pipeline view of every candidate. See who's applied, who's in review, who needs references, and who's ready to start.

Document Management

Candidates upload right-to-work, qualifications, and ID documents during application. No chasing paperwork.

Induction & Onboarding

Structured carer induction with four-nation templates that tick off as each step is completed, so new starters are compliant and confident from day one.

Reference Management

Request, track, and store references digitally. Automatic reminders to referees who haven't responded.

Direct to System

Approved applicants flow straight into your carer database. No re-keying, no duplicate data entry.

Compliance-Ready

DBS application tracking, training requirements flagged from day one, and regulatory registration checks built into the flow.

Recruiting from overseas?

Sponsor-Licence Compliance, Built In

If you hold (or are applying for) a Worker sponsor licence and recruit under the Health and Care Worker visa, homecareOS handles the compliance trail too: Certificate of Sponsorship allocation, Right to Work checks, visa expiry alerts, and the 10-day UKVI reporting events you have to file via the Sponsorship Management System.
