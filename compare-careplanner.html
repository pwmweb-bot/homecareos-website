The short answer Is CarePlanner still the right choice for a UK home care agency?

CarePlanner is a capable rostering system with over a decade in UK home care and more than 2,000 domiciliary agencies on its books, but choosing it today means choosing the Nourish roadmap. Nourish acquired CarePlanner in October 2023, and the combined home care product is now developed under the wider Nourish suite, so care planning depth comes from pairing two platforms rather than one. homecareOS takes the opposite approach: drag and drop scheduling, digital care records with eMAR, four-nation compliance for CQC, CIW, Care Inspectorate and RQIA, finance, and an offline carer app are all built into a single system. Pricing differs too. CarePlanner charges per user, on enquiry, while the alternative here is a per-carer licence at free for your first 5 carers, then £10 per carer per month with office staff unmetered and a free starter tier under 5 carers.

6 Reasons Agencies Choose homecareOS Over CarePlanner

CarePlanner's rostering is genuinely strong, it built a large install base on it. Here is where homecareOS pulls ahead for agencies choosing software in 2026.

Architecture Advantage

One Platform, One Roadmap

CarePlanner was scheduling-first, with care planning depth arriving later through the Nourish acquisition. homecareOS was designed as a single system from day one: rota, care records, compliance and finance share one data model, not a suite stitched together.

Pricing Advantage

One Plan, Per Active Carer

CarePlanner prices per user, on enquiry. homecareOS is one simple per-carer licence: Start free for your first 5 carers, then £10 per carer per month, with your whole office team unmetered. Scheduling, care records, compliance and finance are all included, and there's a free starter tier for agencies under 5 carers.

Unique to homecareOS

Bradford Factor Scoring

Automatic absence pattern analysis (S² × D) with RAG thresholds. homecareOS calculates Bradford Factor automatically for every staff member.

Operational AI

AI Cover Finder

When a carer calls in sick, homecareOS scores available carers on distance, continuity, hours headroom, preferences, and schedule fit to find cover in seconds. CarePlanner's rostering is manual-first, coordinators do the searching.

Day One Feature

Four-Nation Compliance

homecareOS supports all four UK nations (CQC, CIW, Care Inspectorate, and RQIA), built into the core data model. Compliance frameworks, background check types, and care plan schemas all follow your nation automatically.

Family Experience

Family Portal with Missed-Visit Alerts

homecareOS provides a full family portal with real-time visit confirmations, carer profiles, care summaries, and instant alerts to family when a visit is missed.
