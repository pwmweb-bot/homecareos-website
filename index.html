THE CHALLENGE

Running a Home Care Agency Shouldn't Be This Hard

Juggling spreadsheets, paper rotas, and disconnected tools wastes time and puts compliance at risk.

Scattered Systems

Most UK home care agencies run their rota, care records, compliance tracking, and invoicing in three or four separate systems that don't talk to each other, typically a scheduling tool, a paper or PDF-based care planning process, a separate compliance spreadsheet, and standalone accounting software. That gap between systems is where most preventable errors happen: a carer's availability changes in the rota tool but nobody updates the care plan, a missed visit doesn't trigger a family alert, or a training certificate expires without anyone noticing until a CQC inspector asks for it. homecareOS replaces that patchwork with a single visit-centric record: one visit connects the carer, the care plan, the compliance evidence, and the invoice line, so a change in one place is reflected everywhere else automatically. For a 30-carer agency, coordinators typically report going from checking four systems each morning to one.

Compliance Anxiety

Keeping up with CQC regulations and ensuring every visit is documented correctly is stressful and time-consuming without the right tools.

Wasted Time

Hours lost on admin tasks, manual data entry, and chasing paperwork instead of focusing on what matters most, delivering quality care.

HOW IT WORKS

From Sign-Up to Go Live in 9 Steps

No complicated setup. No consultant fees. Just follow the steps and you're running.

Create Your Agency

Set up in under 2 minutes, verify your agency via Companies House, no sales call. Your office team (coordinators, managers, owners) is unmetered.

Import Your Data

We help you import data from your existing provider, even if they are slow to help, or not helpful at all. Simply upload whatever you can export, print or access and we'll get it into the system for you.

Create Carers & Service Users

Add your team and the people they care for. Profiles, contact details, care needs, medications, risk assessments, everything in one place from day one.

Create Visits

Build your rota with drag-and-drop scheduling. Set recurring patterns, assign carers, and let the system handle travel time and conflict detection.

Invite Your Team

Send invites to your carers, family members, and the rest of the office team. Everyone gets their own purpose-built portal, no training needed.

Push the Big GO LIVE Button!

When you're ready, hit Go Live. Your agency is now running on homecareOS. Your dashboard lights up with real-time data from the very first visit.

Carers Hit the Road

Your carers see their visits on the mobile app and go about their normal day. Any support they need is right there on their phone, or they can contact homecareOS support via text, email, message, call, or our AI assistant.

Stay on Top of Alerts

Real-time alerts keep your office informed the moment something needs attention. Late visits, missed check-ins, schedule changes, react with confidence, not guesswork.

Invoices, Payroll & Compliance

Generate invoices, run payroll, and produce CQC compliance reports with a few clicks. Everything is already captured from the visits, no double entry, no spreadsheets.
