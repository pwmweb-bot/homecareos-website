The Phone System Built Into Your Home Care Software

Every other platform tells you to buy your phone system somewhere else, then glue the two together. homecareOS puts a full UK phone system inside the software that already runs your rota and your records. Every call is logged in the same place as the care records. Out of hours, the number diverts to your on-call mobile and anything unanswered comes back as a transcribed voicemail. Check our comparison pages: of the sixteen UK platforms we compare, not one builds the phone system in.

PHONE SYSTEM & COMMS

One number your whole agency answers, wherever they are

A UK landline your coordinators answer from a browser, at the office or at their kitchen table. An IVR that greets every caller and routes them to the right team. Voicemail transcribed before the morning rush. And because the phone lives inside the care platform, the call log, the voicemail and its transcript all sit in the software an inspector is already reading.

Why built in beats bolted on

What an integrated phone system does that a separate one never can

A standalone VoIP provider gives you a number and a queue, then keeps the record of it on somebody else's system. Yours sits in the same software as the rota, the care records and the invoices.

Every call logged in one place

Calls in and out are logged with the number, the time, the direction and how long they lasted, and a voicemail arrives with the recording and the transcript attached. When a safeguarding question is asked six months later, you search one call log inside homecareOS, sitting alongside the visit notes from the same day. No phone bill cross-referencing, no “who spoke to the daughter in March”.

Out-of-hours cover on the same number

Set your office hours once. Outside them the number diverts to the on-call mobile, and if that phone does not answer the caller leaves a voicemail that is transcribed and waiting in the morning. Nobody hands out a personal mobile number, and families keep ringing the number they already know.

One vendor, one bill, one login, no integration project

No PBX hardware, no headset contracts, no telecoms reseller, and no six-week project to wire a phone API into your care system. Add a number to your account and answer it in the browser the same day. Outbound calls go from the softphone in the top bar, so nobody leaves the screen they are working on.

Watch a call move through your agency

Office hours: the caller presses one, reaches the care team, and the call is logged as it happens. Out of hours: the same number goes straight to the on-call mobile, and an unanswered call becomes a voicemail that is transcribed, emailed and waiting to be marked handled before the morning rush.

Menus, departments, on-call divert and voicemail transcription are configured per agency. Available on any Pro plan.

The phone system in detail

A phone system built around home care calls

A family ringing at quarter to seven. A carer who cannot get an answer at the door. A commissioner chasing an invoice query. The phone system is shaped around those, not around a generic contact centre.

A UK number that lives in the browser

Take incoming calls and place outbound ones from the coordinator’s screen, with mute and a running call timer while you are connected. No handset on the desk, no personal mobile doubling as the out-of-hours line, and no separate phone bill to reconcile against the rota.

Every call logged where the care records are

A call is logged with the number, the time, the direction and how long it lasted, so the record of a conversation sits in the same software as the record of what was done that day. When a safeguarding question is asked six months later, the call log is in homecareOS, not with a separate phone supplier.

Menus, departments and on-call divert

Callers reach the right team rather than whoever happens to pick up. Departments, IVR menus and agent status are configured in the platform, and outside office hours the line diverts to your on-call mobile.

Voicemail you read instead of listen to

Messages left overnight are transcribed and emailed to the address you nominate, so a coordinator triages the morning’s voicemail by scanning it rather than working through it.

Text alerts to the phone people answer

A missed visit or a shift with nobody on it can go out as a text as well as a push notification and an email. Alerts go one way. The conversation that follows happens on the phone, or in the platform’s own messaging where it is logged.

Care messaging inside the platform, off personal phones

Individual and group threads held inside the platform where they are auditable, with an unread count on every conversation so a message is not left sitting. Care information never sits on a personal device the agency cannot see, and access ends the day someone leaves.

Broadcast when the weather closes a round

One message to every carer at once. Snow, a road closure, or a change everybody needs to know before the morning calls, sent once rather than rung round.
