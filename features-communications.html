A Phone System Built for Home Care

A UK phone number in your browser, an IVR that routes calls intelligently, voicemail transcribed to text, on-call diversion to mobile, two-way SMS, secure team messaging and family/commissioner portals — all in one platform. VoIP available on Pro plans.

PHONE SYSTEM & COMMS

One Number. One Inbox. Everything Logged.

Most agencies stitch together a desk phone, a mobile diverted out of hours, a WhatsApp group, a forgotten email account and a paper handover book. homecareOS replaces all of it. Get a UK landline number you can answer from the browser, an automated IVR that asks "press 1 for emergency", voicemails transcribed straight to your dashboard, and SMS conversations stored against the right service user record. Plus secure team messaging, family portal and commissioner portal — already wired in.

Purpose-Built for Care

Communication That Actually Works in Care

Every channel is designed around the real communication pressures of running a home care agency — not generic business messaging.

In-App Messaging

Secure, GDPR-compliant messaging between office staff and carers. Individual and group conversations with read receipts.

Push Notifications

Instant alerts for schedule changes, new visits, cancelled visits, and urgent messages. Carers never miss critical updates.

VoIP Telephony

Built-in phone system for your office. Call carers and families directly from the platform. Call logs linked to visit records.

Automated Alerts

System-generated notifications for late visits, missed check-ins, training expiry, and compliance deadlines.

Family Communication

Secure messaging channel between families and the agency. No more playing phone tag with worried relatives.

Handover Notes

Structured digital handovers between shifts and team members. Critical information passed on reliably, not verbally.

PRO PLAN

The Full VoIP Phone System

Available on any Pro plan. Add a UK phone number to your homecareOS account and answer it from any browser. No headset deals, no PBX hardware, no separate vendor — just a proper care-grade phone system inside the platform you already use.

📞

UK Number in Your Browser

Pick a 01, 02 or 03 number for your area (or port your existing landline). Answer it from any laptop, tablet or phone — no desk phone required.

🔀

Smart IVR Routing

"Press 1 for emergency cover, press 2 for rota, press 3 for invoicing." Build your own menus visually. Out-of-hours options auto-switch on a schedule.

📝

Voicemail Transcription

Every voicemail transcribed to text the moment it lands. Skim your messages instead of listening to all 14. Critical voicemails flagged for follow-up.

📱

On-Call Mobile Diversion

Out of hours, calls divert to whichever carer is on call this weekend. Rotation lives in the rota. No more sharing personal mobile numbers.

💬

Two-Way SMS

Send and receive text messages from your UK number. Stored against the service user record so the next coordinator sees the full thread.

🔍

Linked to Visits

Every call and SMS linked to the relevant carer, service user or family contact. One audit trail, not five different inboxes.

Full Feature List

Complete Communication Platform

Every communication channel your agency needs — messaging, telephony, notifications and handovers — in one secure, auditable system.

Secure in-app messaging

End-to-end secure messaging that keeps all care-related communication within a GDPR-compliant platform — not on personal devices or unmanaged apps.

Individual and group conversations

Message a single carer privately or create team channels for specific runs, regions, or roles — keeping the right people informed without noise.

Read receipts and delivery confirmation

Know exactly when a message has been delivered and read. No more guessing whether your carer received the schedule change.

Push notification system (VAPID)

Web push notifications powered by VAPID keys deliver instant alerts to carers' devices — even when the app isn't open.

VoIP telephony integration

Built-in phone system lets office staff call carers and families directly from the platform. No personal numbers, no separate phone bills.

Call logging and recording

Every call is logged against the relevant carer, service user, or visit — creating a complete audit trail for compliance and safeguarding.

Automated system alerts

The platform generates alerts automatically when events require attention — no coordinator has to remember to send them manually.

Late visit notifications

When a carer hasn't checked in on time, the office is alerted immediately — and families can be notified automatically too.

Training expiry reminders

Automated reminders when training certificates, DBS checks, or professional registrations are approaching expiry — sent to both the carer and their manager.

Family messaging portal

Families get a secure, dedicated channel to communicate with the agency — asking questions, raising concerns, or simply checking in on their loved one.

Digital handover notes

Structured handover templates ensure critical information is passed between shifts reliably — not scribbled on paper or mentioned in passing.

Broadcast messages to teams

Send a single message to an entire team, run, or region instantly — perfect for weather alerts, policy updates, or urgent care changes.
