Why we made it

An informed buyer makes a better decision

Choosing home care software is one of the biggest operational decisions an agency makes. It shapes how coordinators build the rota, how carers record what happened at a visit, how you evidence compliance to your regulator, and how quickly you get paid. Switching later is painful, so it pays to choose well the first time.

We are homecareOS, so we have a horse in this race, and we say so plainly in the guide. But the scorecard, the questions and the worksheet work whichever product you pick. We would rather you choose the right platform with your eyes open than be sold the wrong one.
