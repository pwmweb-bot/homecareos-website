Home / Compliance / CIW inspection ready

Built for RISCA, not translated from England

CIW rates a domiciliary support service against three themes, not four, and homecareOS knows the difference. Every line of enquiry that applies to you has an evidence route, drawn from the records your team already keeps.

What homecareOS does

The evidence is gathered while you work

Not a compliance module you visit once a year. Four things that run continuously, from the day you switch your nation on.

Evidence assembles itself

Twenty one rules watch the records you already keep. A completed training course, a falls risk assessment, an incident closed with learning, a right to work check: each writes a link to the framework item it evidences, at the moment it is filed. Nobody tags anything, and nothing is copied into a separate folder that then drifts.

Every date an inspector asks about

Care plan reviews, risk assessment reviews and the trigger that forced them, training expiry, background check renewals, supervisions, complaint response deadlines, audits, incident investigations and goal reviews. All tracked, all chaseable, all in one overdue view.

Records that cannot be quietly tidied

A care note or a MAR entry cannot be edited or deleted once written. Medicines record taken, refused or not administered as three different clinical events, with who recorded them and when. That immutability is the thing that makes the rest of your evidence worth showing.

Your nation, not a translation

Set your nation and the whole platform follows: the right regulator on printed documents, the right registration and background check vocabulary, the right mandatory training list, and the right ethnicity classification, which genuinely differs across the four nations.

CIW’s framework

All 12 lines of enquiry, and evidence linked to 12 of them

CIW’s inspection framework, across the three themes that apply to home care. homecareOS holds every one and applies the set that matches your nation.

3Well-being
4Care and Support
5Leadership and Management

Records you already keep are matched to the lines of enquiry they evidence as they are filed, through fourteen concepts: risk management, medicines safety, training, supervision, safe recruitment, safeguarding, infection prevention, governance and audit, complaints, person-centred planning, family involvement, people’s experience, outcomes and falls. For Wales that reaches 12 of 12, 100%.

Every line of enquiry that applies to a domiciliary support service has at least one evidence route. That is unusual, and it is because CIW writes its lines of enquiry around what a service actually does rather than around culture.

CIW works to four inspection themes, but its own framework limits Environment to “accommodation-based services only”, so a domiciliary support service is inspected and rated against three. LOE 12 exists for domiciliary support services only, and homecareOS carries it. Getting that right matters: it is the most commonly repeated error in vendor copy about Wales.

Know what to expect

What CIW has written about other agencies

Three findings from published CIW reports on domiciliary services, so you can check yourself against them before someone else does. Illustrations rather than a frequency table: the sample is small and weighted towards services that were struggling.

Stays with you

Three things to keep hold of yourself

Good software makes these easier to see and harder to forget. It does not do them for you, and an inspector will know the difference.

The Responsible Individual’s own visits

We hold the three-monthly meeting record and the six-monthly quality of care review, and chase both when they fall due. The conversations themselves, and the judgement in them, are the RI’s.

The Active Offer

Recording that someone wants their care in Welsh is a field, and we hold it. Rostering a Welsh-speaking carer to them is a staffing decision only you can make.

Getting value from the system you bought

CIW has written up a service whose call monitoring was in place but underused. Software you do not work is a finding waiting to happen, which is why remote training is in the Pro licence rather than sold as an extra.
