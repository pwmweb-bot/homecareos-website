Person-centred care plan

A free template for home care (domiciliary care) agencies. Write it with the person, in their words where you can, and put the summary at the front. Adapt it to your own policies and your regulator's requirements.

Before you start
  • Avoid vague verbs like "assist" or "support" on their own. Say what the carer actually does, and how the person likes it done.
  • Record risks the person chooses to take, and what has been agreed, rather than quietly stopping things.
  • Be exact about medicines: none, prompt, assist or administer, at which call.
  • Agree a review date. NICE NG21 recommends a first review within 6 weeks, then at least annually.

1One-page summary

The five or six things every carer must know before they start. Keep it to what fits here.

2About me

Who I am, in my own words where possible: my history, family, work, what I enjoy, what makes me laugh, what I would like you to call me.

3What matters to me

My priorities, in my words. Not the agency's.

So nobody takes over. Note anything that changes with time of day, tiredness or how I am feeling.
