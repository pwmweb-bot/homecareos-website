homecareOS Growth Stage

Built for Home Care

One Platform, Every Tool

Unlike generic business software, homecareOS was designed from the ground up specifically for UK home care agencies. Every feature, every workflow, and every compliance check is tailored to the sector.

  • Real-time carer location tracking
  • Automated invoicing and payroll preparation
