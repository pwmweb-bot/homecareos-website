You can think about leaving QuikPlan without having to figure the move out alone

A lot of owners and managers have had the thought for months. The rota still has to run on Monday, the office already knows the screens, and a move feels like the kind of disruption you cannot afford in a busy week. If that is where you are, start with a conversation. We migrate your current carers, service users, care plans and rota for free, and you are working in HomecareOS within 7 days of sending us your exports.

You're not alone

Switching feels hard for ordinary reasons

The thing that keeps agencies on software they have outgrown is rarely the software. It is the fear of the move. If QuikPlan has been the system your coordinators know, that familiarity is worth something. Putting the decision off is a reasonable response to the week you are already having.

The risk

Records are how you evidence care. The worry is that a transfer lands them in the wrong place, or that you only find out on the morning you needed them.

The time

Asking a provider for an export has a way of quietly using up a notice period. Most people leave that request too late, then feel stuck.

The staff

Coordinators and carers already know today's screens. Teaching another system feels like one more job on a full week, so the idea stays on the list.

The data

People worry the history of the agency is locked in someone else's system, and that leaving means starting the rota from a blank page.

Live in 7 days

The seven days start when your exports arrive

From the day we receive your exports, your live carers, service users, care plans and rota are loaded, checked with you and ready to work from within 7 days. The order below is the same every time. Field names and date formats differ from one file to the next, so we do not pretend each morning has a fixed job.

How we minimise risk

You are not handed an importer and wished well

Today a real person does this with you. The first thing you agree is a written migration plan. Self-service migration is on the roadmap. It is not how it works today.

A written plan, agreed first

We agree a written migration plan with you before any data moves, so the shape of the switch is settled before the work starts.

We run the migration

You send the exports, we map the fields, you check the preview, and we commit it.

Live data moves free

Current carers, current service users, their care plans and the running rota. There is no charge for that, and there is no setup fee.

Checked before you go live

You see a preview before anything is committed, then we check the load with you. It is ready to work from within those 7 days.

No contract minimum

The software has no annual contract minimum. History, if you want it, is quoted before any of that work starts, and it is optional: keeping your previous provider's export can meet your retention duty.

There is also no size limit. The licence prices carers rather than capping them, so an import is never refused because your agency is too big or your history too long. The fuller account of what moves, and what happens if an import needs to be reversed, is on the migration page.

What moves across

The live records you need on Monday morning

Not a flat dump of contacts. Visits stay attached to the right service user and the right carer.

Carers

Current carers, with contact details and start dates. Worker registrations for SCW, SSSC and NISCC come across with them, along with training records and background checks such as DBS.

Service users

Current service users, with addresses and care details. Past service users are history, which is a separate, quoted job and does not hold up the switch.

Care plans

Care plans come across with the people they belong to. Care notes and medication (MAR) history are not imported. They stay in your previous provider's export, which is also how your retention duty can be met.

Rota

The running rota, so you can carry on working. Visits come across too, still attached to the right service user and the right carer. Old visits are part of history, and that part is quoted before it starts.
