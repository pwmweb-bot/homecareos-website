homecareOS vs QuikPlan

QuikPlan has supported UK home care agencies for over 20 years and is widely respected for hands-on UK-based support and free unlimited training. homecareOS is a newer, AI-native build, a different generation with different trade-offs. Here's an honest comparison.

Credit Where It's Due

Who Are QuikPlan?

QuikPlan has been serving UK home care agencies for over 20 years. That's a long time to refine what good rostering and finance software looks like, and it shows. They're a UK-based SME with a reputation for genuinely caring about their customers, agencies routinely highlight their free unlimited training and UK-based human support as standout strengths. If you've spoken to QuikPlan customers, you'll have heard this.

Their cloud platform offers visit rostering with calendar and grid views, a Template Manager for recurring schedules, and a Task Manager for visit-level task assignment. The QuikApp mobile app supports visit times, tasks, eMAR, notes, and incident reporting. QPLive! provides real-time visit monitoring, QuikDocs handles document management, and the platform includes invoicing, staff pay, client and staff portals, maps with mileage and travel tracking, and around 100 standard reports. Compliance covers CQC, RQIA, Care Inspectorate, CIW, and HIQA.

Where the difference lies is generational. Any platform that's been around for two decades carries a degree of technical debt, not a criticism, just the reality of long-running software. It makes shipping newer paradigms (AI-driven scheduling, modern mobile-first UX, fast iteration on emerging features) slower. homecareOS made the opposite trade-off: a newer codebase with less proven longevity, in exchange for being able to ship AI cover finding, AI rota building, Bradford Factor analytics, and richer care records out of the box.

Neither approach is universally right. It depends what you value.

When QuikPlan might be the better fit

  • You strongly value 20+ years of proven track record over newer-but-faster-evolving software
  • Hands-on, included-as-standard UK training and support is non-negotiable
  • Your priority is rock-solid rostering and finance, with care planning handled elsewhere
  • You prefer to pay for software and have the supplier relationship that comes with that

If those describe you, QuikPlan is a genuinely strong choice and we'd say so. If you'd rather have AI scheduling, Bradford Factor analytics, deeper digital care records, and transparent per-active-carer pricing as you grow, that's where homecareOS fits.
