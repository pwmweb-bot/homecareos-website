homecareOS vs Access Group

Access is enterprise-grade with deep NHS integration. But enterprise comes with enterprise complexity and cost. homecareOS delivers the power without the price tag.

Pricing structure We charge per carer. Access charges per user — plus implementation.

On Access, every coordinator, manager and back-office user adds to your bill — on top of £5k–£25k implementation fees and three-year contracts. On homecareOS, only active carers count: your office team is unmetered, there's no implementation fee, no contract minimum, and you're live in days not weeks.
