Architecture

Purpose-Built for Dom Care

homecareOS was designed from the ground up for domiciliary care workflows — scheduling, travel time, lone working, community-based compliance. PCS is residential-first, with domiciliary bolted on.

Scheduling

Drag-and-Drop Rota

Canvas-rendered time grid handling 80 carers and 480 visits at 60fps. Drag, drop, resize, reassign — all in real time. PCS offers partial scheduling that wasn't built for high-volume community rotas.

Unique to homecareOS

AI Cover Finder

Scores available carers on distance (30%), continuity (25%), hours headroom (20%), preferred carer (15%), and schedule fit (10%). Finds ideal cover in seconds. homecareOS includes this as standard.

Bradford Factor Scoring

Automatic absence pattern analysis (S² × D) with RAG thresholds — 0–99 green, 100–199 amber, 200+ red. homecareOS includes this as standard.

Day One Feature

CQC, CIW, Care Inspectorate, and RQIA built in from day one. Serve agencies across England, Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland. PCS has limited multi-nation support.

Pricing

Simple, published pricing: Start at £7/active carer/month and Pro at £10/active carer/month, with your whole office team unmetered. There's also a free starter tier for agencies under 5 active carers. PCS charges licence fees for their Connected Care Platform, with pricing on request.

About the Competitor

Who Are Person Centred Software?

Person Centred Software (PCS) is the market leader in residential care technology, trusted by over 6,000 care homes across the UK. Their Connected Care Platform covers digital care planning, eMAR, and compliance — primarily designed for residential and nursing home settings.

PCS also offers an eLearning platform and runs weekly webinars. They are NHS Assured and have a training portal for onboarding, though their public-facing help centre is more limited compared to some competitors.

Their domiciliary care offering exists but is secondary to their residential focus. For agencies whose entire operation is community-based home care, a purpose-built platform like homecareOS is a stronger fit.
