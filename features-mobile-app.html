A purpose-built, mobile-first app for UK home care that works offline, giving your care team everything they need at the point of care, even in areas with no signal.

CARER MOBILE APP

Built for Carers, Not Against Them

Most care software treats the mobile app as an afterthought, a stripped-down version of the desktop built to tick a box. homecareOS was different from day one. We built the carer mobile experience as a core part of the platform, designed around how carers actually work in the field: one-handed, between visits, often with patchy signal. The result is an app your carers will genuinely want to use.

THE CARER EXPERIENCE

Everything a Carer Needs, Right in Their Pocket

Tap a tab to explore each feature, designed for one-handed use between visits.

Visit Details & Navigation

Every visit shows the service user, address, scheduled time, and one-tap navigation. Key safe codes are hidden behind a tap for security. Carers know exactly where they're going and what to expect before they arrive.

Visit Action Hub

One screen with every action a carer needs during a visit: check in, record tasks, administer medications, add notes, log observations, capture body maps, take photos, and update goals. No hunting through menus.

Clinical Observations

Record Bristol Stool charts, fluid intake, food intake, repositioning, mood and wellbeing, seizures, vital signs, sleep, personal care, and home environment assessments, all with structured templates that feed directly into the care record.

Body Map Recording

Tap to mark wounds, bruises, or skin concerns on a front or back body outline. Each mark records the date, carer, and notes, building a visual history that's invaluable for safeguarding and clinical handovers.

Care Goal Tracking

Track progress against health, safety, and wellbeing goals with visual progress bars and target dates. Carers update goals during visits, giving managers and families real-time visibility into outcomes, not just task completion.

Pay Transparency

Carers see their projected gross pay, hours worked, rate breakdowns, bank holiday rates, and mileage, updated in real time. No more end-of-month surprises. Transparency builds trust and reduces payroll queries by up to 80%.

Team Messaging

Secure, GDPR-compliant messaging between carers and the office, replacing WhatsApp groups and personal texts. Conversations are logged, searchable, and tied to the agency, not someone's personal phone number.

Leave Requests

Carers submit annual leave, request time off, and track approval status directly from the app. Managers approve or decline with one tap. No more paper forms, lost emails, or coordinators finding out about leave too late.
