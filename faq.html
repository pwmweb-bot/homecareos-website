Getting started and switching

Setting up, moving your data across and getting your team trained.

Who is HomecareOS for?

HomecareOS is built for UK domiciliary care agencies, where carers travel to people in their own homes. It works for agencies in England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, and follows the regulator for your nation.

It is not designed for residential care homes or nursing homes. If that is your service, Person Centred Software or Nourish are better suited.

How do I get started?

Create an account in a couple of minutes. It is free while you have up to 5 carers, with no credit card and no time limit, so you can add your carers and service users, build a real rota and run live visits before you pay anything.

If you would rather see it first, book a 15-minute demo or call Phil on 01905 674704.

How long does setup take?

Creating the account takes minutes. On the Free plan you also get a 30 minute remote overview to get your carers, service users and first week of rota in, and put the app on a carer's phone.

If you are moving from another system, we move your live data for you and aim to have you switched within about 7 days of receiving your exports. How quickly your team settles in depends on how many coordinators and carers you have, so we plan training around your rota rather than a fixed timetable.

Can I migrate easily to HomecareOS?

Yes. We run the migration for you rather than handing you an importer. Your live data, meaning current carers, service users, care plans and the running rota, comes across free from a CSV export, with relationships preserved. If something is not right, the whole import is reversed in one step rather than unpicked by hand.

Historic records, such as past service users, former staff and old visit history, are optional and quoted separately, because the price depends on what your current provider will actually hand over. We look at a sample first at no charge. See how a migration runs.

My current provider will not let me have my data. What can I do?

Your agency is the data controller for its care records, and your software provider processes them on your behalf. Under UK GDPR, a processor must delete or return personal data to the controller at the end of the service, at the controller's choice, and your contract should say how that happens.

Ask your provider in writing for a full export, and check your contract for notice periods and export terms. We are happy to look at what they send and tell you what we can bring across.

Do you offer training?

Yes. Free includes a 30 minute remote overview, then support by email and chat. Pro includes live remote training for your office team and your carers, and it is available again when you take on new coordinators or change how you work. Onsite training at your office sits outside the licence and is quoted before anything is booked.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes. HomecareOS is free for your first 5 carers, with no credit card and no time limit. Your office team and service users are unmetered. Once you go past 5 carers, Pro is £10 per carer per month. You can also book a demo for a guided tour first.

Do carers need special phones or equipment?

No. The carer app is free for every carer and runs on the iPhone or Android phone they already have. Your office team uses HomecareOS in a web browser, so there is nothing to install on office computers. If you choose NFC tags or printed QR codes for clocking in, those are the only extra items you need.

Pricing and plans

What it costs, what each plan includes and how billing works.

How much does HomecareOS cost?

It is free while you have up to 5 carers. Once you go past 5, it is £10 per carer per month on Pro, for every carer (6 carers = £60, 20 = £200). Coordinators, managers and owners who do not deliver visits are never counted, and there is no cap on service users. Annual billing saves a further 20%. See pricing for the calculator.

What is included in the Free plan?

The full core platform: drag-and-drop rota scheduling, digital care plans, the carer mobile app with GPS check-in and check-out, care notes and visit logging, medication (MAR) records, incident and safeguarding management, training and DBS expiry tracking, invoicing, billing and payroll exports, the family portal and Cover Finder, reporting, and the compliance frameworks for all four UK nations. Your office team and service users are unmetered.

What does Pro add?

Everything in Free, plus the AI Rota Builder, advanced analytics and benchmarking, the custom report builder, the commissioner portal, white-label branding, the integrations hub with roadmap voting, the VoIP phone system, Care Conversations, a dedicated account manager, remote training for your team, and priority support with an SLA.

How is the number of carers counted?

Headcount is taken at your renewal date, so a carer who joins mid-cycle is billed from the next cycle, and leavers are not charged after they go. Office staff who do not deliver visits are never counted.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Plans are month-to-month and you can cancel at any time with no fees. Annual billing gives you a 20% discount. You can upgrade or downgrade from your account: upgrades take effect straight away, and downgrades from the start of your next billing cycle.

Are there any setup or migration fees?

There is no charge to bring your live data across. Historic records are optional and quoted after we have looked at a sample for free. Onsite training is quoted before anything is booked. We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express through Stripe, and annual plans can be paid by BACS bank transfer on request.

How much does home care management software cost in the UK?

There are three common pricing models, and they produce very different bills for the same agency. Per user charges for everyone with a login, so coordinators, managers and office staff all add cost. Per service user charges by the number of people you support. Per carer charges only for the staff who deliver visits. HomecareOS is free while you have up to 5 carers and then £10 per carer per month, with office staff and service users never counted. Ask any vendor to price your actual headcount rather than quoting a headline figure.

How is pricing calculated for care planning software?

Almost always per user, per service user or per carer, charged monthly, with an annual option at a discount. Watch for three things that change the real figure: whether office staff count as users, whether modules such as eMAR, scheduling or a family portal are extra, and whether there is a minimum contract or a setup fee. The question to ask is not the rate but the total for your exact headcount in twelve months' time.

Features

What HomecareOS does today. If something is not listed here or on the feature pages, ask us before you rely on it.

How do care plans work?

Each person's plan is written in sections that follow your regulator, such as about me, personal care, mobility, nutrition, medication and communication, and each section has its own review date. Tasks show which calls they belong to, and risks, medicines and outcome goals link back to the part of the plan they belong to.

Carers read the relevant parts on their phone during the visit, and you can preview exactly what a carer will see before you publish a change. On Pro, Care Conversations drafts plan sections from a recorded assessment conversation for your team to check and edit. See care planning.

Can I build a regular weekly rota from templates?

Yes. Each person's regular visits sit on their record as template visits. A coordinator can also save a set of visits as a named run, such as a Monday morning round in one area, then apply it to a carer and a date, and HomecareOS creates every visit at the right times and tells the carer. You adjust the week with drag and drop, and the rota marks problems such as double-ups, clashes and carers working outside their usual availability. See rota and scheduling.

Can HomecareOS handle double-up visits?

Yes. A visit can need two carers, and the rota marks it as a double-up. A genuine 2:1 can carry a different service for each carer on the same visit, so it bills and pays correctly for each of them rather than pretending to be two identical calls.

Can different visits for the same person be billed to different payers?

Yes. What you charge is set by the payer, the service and the rate, so one person's calls can be billed to different payers, such as the council, NHS continuing healthcare or the person themselves, each on its own terms. What you charge is kept separate from what you pay carers. See finance and billing.

Does HomecareOS allocate carers automatically?

It suggests and you decide. Cover Finder, included on every plan, ranks the carers available for an uncovered visit. On Pro, the AI Rota Builder proposes a rota weighing travel time, continuity, contracted hours, competencies and the person's preferences. Your coordinator reviews the suggestions and confirms them. Nothing is assigned without someone in your office agreeing to it.

What can carers do in the app?

Carers see their visits and the person's care plan, clock in and out, complete tasks, record medication on the eMAR, write care notes, add body map entries and observations, and see their projected pay. Many of these actions work with no signal and send themselves when a connection returns, which matters in homes and areas with poor coverage. The app is free for every carer on iPhone and Android. See the carer app.

Does it include eMAR?

Yes. Medication (MAR) records are on every plan, and carers record each dose on their phone during the visit. Agency staff cannot edit or delete medication records once they are made. See eMAR.

How does electronic call monitoring work?

Carers clock in and out from the app by GPS, by tapping an NFC tag in the home, or by scanning a printed QR code, with an office clock-in for when something has genuinely gone wrong. The method is stamped on every visit, clock-ins are checked against the address, and clocking in and out works offline. Late and missed visits are flagged to the office automatically, using thresholds you set. See call monitoring.

Can carers clock out without finishing their tasks?

You choose. Optional clock-out rules can warn the carer, ask them for a reason, or block clock-out until the required tasks are done.

Is there a family portal?

Yes, on every plan. With the person's agreement, you invite relatives and choose what each one can see, for example visits, care notes, medication, the care plan, the schedule and who is coming next. See the family portal.

Is there a portal for local authority commissioners?

Yes, on Pro. The commissioner portal gives local authorities and NHS commissioners a view of visit completion, hours delivered against hours commissioned, the care records behind each visit, and invoices. See the commissioner portal.

Does it do timesheets and payroll?

Yes. Weekly timesheets are built from actual visit times, with pay grades, enhanced and bank holiday rates, and mileage by road distance between visits. There is a National Minimum Wage check, which currently covers contact time rather than travel time, so treat a pass as a first check. Pay runs export to Sage, Xero and BrightPay. See carer payroll.

What replaces spreadsheets for care agency finance?

The job spreadsheets usually do in a care agency is turning delivered visits into invoices and pay, and that is the part care management software should own end to end. In HomecareOS, visits carry planned, actual, paid and charged time separately, rates come from the payer and service, and invoices and pay runs are generated from the same visit records rather than being re-keyed.

How does invoicing software help a care provider's cash flow?

Mostly by shortening the gap between delivering care and billing for it. If invoices are built from the visits your carers actually clocked, you can bill weekly or fortnightly instead of waiting for someone to reconcile a month of timesheets. It also helps stop under-billing, such as visits that ran long, cancelled calls that are still chargeable, and bank holiday rates nobody applied.

What reports are included?

Reporting is included on every plan, including visit completeness and clock-in reports for contract monitoring. Pro adds advanced analytics and benchmarking and a custom report builder. See reporting.

We run more than one branch. Does that work?

Yes. Every branch you run sits behind one login, with a group dashboard that compares branches on the same measures and lets you drop into any one of them. See multi-branch.

Is there a phone system?

Yes, on Pro. The VoIP phone system gives you a UK number in the coordinator's browser, call menus, departments, on-call divert and voicemail transcription, in the same system as your care records. Live conversations are not recorded. See communications.

Can it track PPE, stock and vehicles?

Yes. HomecareOS keeps a register of PPE and consumables with reorder levels, carer kit issued and returned, agency vehicles, and the MOT, insurance and licence dates for carers' own cars, flagged as they come round. See stock, PPE and vehicles.

What makes HomecareOS different from other home care software?

It is built only for domiciliary care, and it runs the whole agency in one system: care plans, the rota, the carer app, medication, compliance, staff files, the family portal, invoicing and pay. The founder has more than 13 years in home care software, and the product is shaped around the day-to-day problems agency owners and managers face. The simplest way to judge it is to try it free with your own rota.

CQC and compliance

How HomecareOS helps you evidence what regulators look for.

Is HomecareOS CQC compliant?

Strictly speaking no software is, and you should be wary of any vendor who says otherwise. Regulators register and inspect the provider, not the system it runs on, so compliance is something your agency holds and HomecareOS helps you evidence. The platform models the framework for your nation: CQC in England, CIW in Wales, the Care Inspectorate in Scotland and RQIA in Northern Ireland, so the records an inspector asks for are already in the shape they expect.

Which home care software is CQC compliant?

None of it, and that is the honest answer. CQC registers and inspects the provider, not the software. What differs between platforms is how well they help you evidence compliance: whether the care records, medication records, audit trail and staff files are in the shape an inspector asks for, and whether the platform models your nation's regulator rather than assuming CQC. If a vendor's website says its product is CQC compliant, treat that as marketing rather than a fact about your registration.

How does HomecareOS help at inspection?

It keeps the evidence in one place: care plans with review dates, visit records and care notes, medication records, incidents and safeguarding, and staff files with training and DBS expiry dates. Every change to a care record is timestamped and attributed, and a correction to a care note keeps the reason and the original wording. You still need your own policies and a well-run service. See compliance and our CQC inspection guide.

Does home care software need to cover all four UK nations?

Only if you operate in more than one, but it matters more than most agencies expect if you do. The regulator, the background check and the workforce register all differ: CQC with the DBS in England, CIW with the DBS and Social Care Wales in Wales, the Care Inspectorate with the PVG scheme and the SSSC in Scotland, and RQIA with AccessNI and NISCC in Northern Ireland. A platform built around CQC can usually be made to work elsewhere, but you end up maintaining the difference by hand.

Data and security

Where your data is held, who can see it, and what is not certified yet.

Where is my data stored?

Care records and uploaded files are stored in Ireland, on Amazon Web Services through our database provider Supabase. The application's server functions run in Dublin, provided by Vercel. Some features rely on specialist providers for phone calls, text messages, email, maps, payments, notifications and AI, and our privacy policy names them, including those in the United States.

Is my data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256), and the database is backed up automatically every day. Every database table has row-level security, so staff only see records from the agency they work for. Carers see only the people they visit, family logins see only their own relative, and each person's menus follow the permissions of their role. Support staff must give a reason to open your account, and past sessions show in your support access log. See security.

Are you registered with the ICO?

Yes. PsycheMeta Ltd, the company behind HomecareOS, is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ZC101853). Your agency is the data controller for its care records, and PsycheMeta Ltd processes them on your behalf.

Do you have Cyber Essentials, ISO 27001 or NHS assurance?

Not yet, and we would rather say so plainly. We are working towards Cyber Essentials but are not certified, we do not hold ISO 27001, and there has been no independent penetration test yet. HomecareOS has not completed the NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit or a DTAC assessment, and it does not connect to GP Connect.

Our hosting providers, Supabase and Vercel, hold ISO 27001 certification and SOC 2 Type 2 reports, but those cover them, not HomecareOS itself. If your commissioner or DPO has questions, we are happy to talk them through what is in place.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. You can download care plan, MAR and risk assessment PDFs and report CSVs, which also help you answer a subject access request. Your agency remains the data controller, so the data is yours.
