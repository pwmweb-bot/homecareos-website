We Have Answers

Find answers to the most common questions about homecareOS, from migration to compliance and beyond.

Yes! We provide a dedicated migration team to handle every aspect of your transition. Whether you're currently on paper records, spreadsheets, or another software system, we'll manage the entire data transfer from start to finish. Most agencies are fully up and running within 2 weeks with zero data loss. Our team will also provide hands-on training to ensure your staff are confident with the new system before we go live.

homecareOS is a true holistic operating system, not just scheduling software. We combine care plans, compliance tracking, staff management, a family portal, and business intelligence tools into one seamless platform. What truly sets us apart is that homecareOS was built by someone with 13+ years of hands-on experience in the home care industry who understands your real, day-to-day challenges. Every feature is designed to solve actual problems that agency owners and managers face.

Your data belongs to you, always. Under UK GDPR regulations, you have the legal right to request a full copy of all your data from any provider. We can help you navigate this process and have extensive experience working with other providers to ensure a smooth data transfer. If needed, we can assist you with formal data portability requests under Article 20 of GDPR. We have never encountered a situation where we couldn't successfully retrieve a client's data.

Yes, and not only for CQC. homecareOS builds compliance for all four UK regulators into every feature: CQC in England, CIW in Wales, the Care Inspectorate in Scotland and RQIA in Northern Ireland. Your agency's nation drives the right frameworks, documentation and automated reporting, and we keep the platform current as each regulator's rules change, so you never fall behind. Agencies have reached the top inspection ratings, including CQC Outstanding in England, after switching to homecareOS.

Most agencies are fully operational within 1-2 weeks. Our dedicated onboarding team guides you through every step of the process, from initial account configuration and data import to staff training and go-live support. We work around your schedule to minimise disruption, and our team remains available throughout the transition period to answer any questions and resolve any issues quickly.

Yes, comprehensive training and support are included with every plan. All users get access to our extensive knowledge base, video tutorials, and email support. PRO and Enterprise plan holders also receive priority phone support, live video training sessions, and dedicated onboarding assistance with a named account manager. We also run regular webinars covering new features and best practices.

Yes. Every agency starts with a 30-day free trial of the full platform, no credit card required, so you can build real rotas, add your carers, and run live visits before you commit. If you'd like a guided tour first, you can also book a personalised demo with our team. We'll walk you through every feature tailored to your agency's specific needs, and answer all your questions live. There's also a free starter tier for the smallest agencies, under 5 active carers.

Security is our top priority. We use bank-level 256-bit SSL encryption for all data in transit, and AES-256 encryption for data at rest. Daily automated backups ensure your data is never at risk, and all servers are UK-based, keeping your information within UK jurisdiction. We are fully GDPR compliant and undergo regular independent security audits. Role-based access controls ensure that only authorised personnel can view sensitive information.
