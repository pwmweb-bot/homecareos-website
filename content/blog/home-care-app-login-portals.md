  - q: How do I log in to my relative's care app?
    a: Ask the care agency which software it uses and whether it has invited you. Almost every home care family portal is opened by the agency, which adds you to the person's record and sends an invitation by email or text. Use the link or app named in that invitation, then sign in with the details you set up. The software company cannot add you itself.
  - q: Why can't I see the family portal?
    a: The most common reasons are that the agency has not invited you yet, the invitation has expired, the portal is not switched on for that agency, or the person you support has asked for their information not to be shared. Some portals also stop showing records when a person's care ends or is paused. Contact the agency first.
  - q: Can the software company give me access to my relative's records?
    a: No. The care agency controls who can see a person's records, because it is responsible for that information. Vendors' own help pages say access is set up by the provider, so contact the agency, which can check your details and send a new invitation.

Most UK home care agencies use software with three front doors: an office login for coordinators and managers, an app for carers, and a portal or app for families. This page collects the official sign-in links for each, so you can get to the right one first time.

**A note before you start.** HomecareOS is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any of the other companies listed here. All product names and trademarks belong to their owners and are used only to identify their products. Every link was taken from the vendor's own website, help centre or official app store listing and checked on 23 October 2026. HomecareOS cannot give anyone access to another company's software. If you are locked out, contact your care agency or the vendor's support page linked in each section.

- **Office staff** sign in at [app.homecareos.co.uk/login](https://app.homecareos.co.uk/login).
- **Carers** are invited by their office by email. The link in that email opens the homecareOS Carer app if it is installed, or the browser if not, and you set your password there. The app is on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/homecareos-carer/id6763616793) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.psychemeta.homecareos.carer), and carers can also sign in on the web at [app.homecareos.co.uk/carer/login](https://app.homecareos.co.uk/carer/login).
- **Families** are invited by the agency by email, set a password from that email, then sign in at [app.homecareos.co.uk/family/login](https://app.homecareos.co.uk/family/login). The agency chooses what is shared. See [what the Family Portal shows](/features-family-portal.html).
- **Locked out?** Use "Forgotten password" on the sign-in page, or ask your agency's office to send a fresh link.

## Birdie

- **Office login (Agency Hub):** [admin.birdie.care](https://admin.birdie.care/). Birdie's help centre says the Agency Hub uses a one-time "magic link" sent to your email instead of a password.
- **Carer app:** Birdie, on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/birdie-care/id1254394392) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kametventures.qoqoon.familyApp). Carers sign in with the mobile number their care manager has registered.
- **Birdie family portal:** the Birdie Family app, which is the same Birdie app. The agency adds you to the person's care circle and sends a text invitation. You then download the app, enter that mobile number, type in the six-digit code sent by text and choose a four-digit passcode. Birdie's [Family app guide](https://help.birdie.care/en/articles/6006503-the-care-circle-birdie-app-for-family-friends) explains the steps and says Birdie's support team cannot add anyone to a care circle.
- **Locked out?** See Birdie's [login help](https://help.birdie.care/en/articles/15203370-how-can-i-resolve-login-issues-on-birdie). If the app says your number is not registered, ask the agency to check your details.

## OneTouch Health

- **Office login:** [care1.onetouchhealth.net/cm](https://care1.onetouchhealth.net/cm/) or [care2.onetouchhealth.net/cm](https://care2.onetouchhealth.net/cm/), depending on which version your agency is on.
- **Carer app:** OneTouch App on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/onetouch-app/id1490800625) and OneTouch Health on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.onetouchmobile). Carers set a username and password from an activation email sent by their agency.
- **OneTouch family portal:** a web portal. OneTouch's [family access guide](https://onetouchhealthhelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4834430942495-Setting-Up-Family-App-Access-for-a-Client) says the agency adds a family member to the client's profile, the family member gets an activation email, and then signs in at [care1.onetouchhealth.net/cm/family/new](https://care1.onetouchhealth.net/cm/family/new/) or [care2.onetouchhealth.net/cm/family](https://care2.onetouchhealth.net/cm/family/), depending on the agency's version. The same portal can also be used by the person receiving care.
- **Locked out?** Contact your agency. OneTouch's [help centre](https://onetouchhealthhelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us) has guides for carers.

## Access Care Planning

Access Care Planning is part of The Access Group. It was formerly called Mobizio, which is why the web address uses mobizio.com.

- **Office login:** your agency's cloud address, in the form cloudX.mobizio.com, where X is your agency's cloud number. Access's guide to [linking Access Care Planning to Access Evo](https://help-accesscareplanning.theaccessgroup.com/en/articles/12033352-link-access-care-planning-to-access-evo) explains this. Staff whose agency uses Access Evo can also reach it from there.
- **Carer app:** Access Care Planning on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/access-care-planning/id1007226873) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mobizio.mobile). You need a server, username and PIN from your agency.
- **Family access:** next of kin use the same Access Care Planning app. Access's [next of kin login guide](https://help-accesscareplanning.theaccessgroup.com/en/articles/12732549-next-of-kin-log-in) says you give the agency your device ID so it can register your device, and it gives you the server, username and PIN.
- **Locked out?** See [Unable to log in to the Access Care Planning app](https://help-accesscareplanning.theaccessgroup.com/en/articles/11993227-unable-to-login-to-the-access-care-planning-app), or ask your agency.

## Access CM (formerly CM2000)

Access CM is The Access Group's care management and electronic call monitoring system, used by councils and home care providers.

- **Office login:** [acm-login.hsc.accessacloud.com](https://acm-login.hsc.accessacloud.com/), linked from the [Access CM product page](https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/products/cm/).
- **Carer app:** CM Mobile on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/cm-mobile/id1449716751) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ezitracker.mobileworker).
- **Family access:** Access describes a [Client Portal](https://help-accesscaremanagement.theaccessgroup.com/en/articles/11793750-client-portal-guide) where relatives can see visits, medication and journal entries. Ask the agency for the link and sign-in details.
- **Locked out?** Contact your agency or council service.

## Webroster

Webroster is part of The Access Group.

- **Office login:** [webroster.accessacloud.com/webroster](https://webroster.accessacloud.com/webroster/), linked from [Webroster's page on The Access Group website](https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/our-brands/webroster/).
- **Carer app:** Bee on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/bee/id1052805596), which Access describes as an app for Webroster customers only. We did not find a current Android listing.
- **Family access:** we did not find a family portal on Webroster's public pages. Ask the agency.
- **Locked out?** Contact your agency's Webroster administrator.

## PASS (everyLIFE Technologies)

- **Office login:** [passgenius.com/logon](https://www.passgenius.com/logon), linked from the [PASS customer log in page](https://pass.care/customer-redirect/).
- **Carer app:** PASSforcare on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/passforcare/id1479380209) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elt.passforcare).
- **Family portal:** openPASS, on the web at [app.openpass.com/logon](https://app.openpass.com/logon) and as an app on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/openpass/id1078577594) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.elt.openpass). The agency gives you a unique ID number, which you use to [request access](https://app.openpass.com/request_access/step1). See [openPASS for family and friends](https://pass.care/openpass/family-and-friends/).
- **Locked out?** See the [openPASS help centre](https://pass.care/openpass/help/), or ask your agency.

## CareLineLive

- **Office login:** each agency has its own address, such as yourcompanyname.carelinelive.app. CareLineLive's [contact page](https://carelinelive.com/contact/) asks people to use the address their organisation gave them rather than searching for a login page.
- **Carer app:** CareLineLive on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/carelinelive/id1469502605) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=carelinelive3.carer). Carers sign in with the server address, email and password from their agency.
- **Family portal:** the Care Circle, which opens only in a web browser. You get an email invitation, which is valid for a week, and create a password. After that you sign in at the agency's own CareLineLive address, so bookmark it. See [Accessing the Care Circle](https://support.carelinelive.com/hc/en-us/articles/5947847074335-Accessing-the-Care-Circle).
- **Locked out?** See [Trouble logging in?](https://support.carelinelive.com/hc/en-us/articles/5947909673375-Trouble-logging-in), or ask your agency to reset your password.

## If you can't get in

Whatever the software, the same few steps solve most problems.

1. **Check you have the right door.** Office logins, carer apps and family portals are separate. A carer login will often not work on the office website, and some family portals only open in a web browser.
2. **Use the link or address from your invitation.** Several systems give each agency its own web address. Bookmark it the first time you sign in.
3. **Ask for a new invitation.** Invitations and one-time links expire. The agency can send a new one.
4. **Contact the agency, not the software company.** The agency decides who can see a person's records, and vendors' own help pages say they cannot add family members themselves.
