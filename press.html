Boilerplate

About homecareOS, in three lengths

Approved copy you can lift as it stands. Take whichever length fits your word count.

25 words

homecareOS is UK home care management software covering rota scheduling, digital care records, eMAR, compliance and invoicing for agencies across all four UK nations.

50 words

homecareOS is home care management software for UK agencies. It combines drag-and-drop rota scheduling, digital care plans and eMAR, an offline-first carer app with GPS-verified visits, a family portal, and invoicing, with compliance built in for CQC, CIW, the Care Inspectorate and RQIA. Made by PsycheMeta Ltd, Worcester.

100 words

homecareOS is home care management software for UK domiciliary care agencies, made by PsycheMeta Ltd in Worcester. Coordinators plan visits on a drag-and-drop rota with travel-time awareness and an AI cover finder. Carers use an offline-first mobile app on iOS and Android with GPS-verified check-in and check-out, digital care plans, care notes, eMAR and body maps. Families follow visits through their own portal. The platform handles invoicing, payroll-ready exports, recruitment, Bradford Factor absence scoring and sponsor licence tracking, with compliance built in for all four UK nations. Pricing is per carer, and office staff are unmetered.

Founder

Philip Martin, Founder

Philip Martin is the founder of homecareOS and an independent care-sector advisor. He has spent more than 13 years building home care software and knows the sector from the inside. He holds an MBA from Wrexham University, where his dissertation asked how to build a sustainable future for home care. He has no first degree: he was accepted onto the Masters on his industry experience and two letters of recommendation. He built homecareOS to give agencies one system for scheduling, care records, compliance and billing.

Available for comment on home care funding, the care workforce and technology in the sector. He can usually turn a quote around the same day, and is happy to do background briefings on the record or off it.
