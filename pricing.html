Home care software pricing: starts at £49 per month + VAT

HomecareOS starts at £49 per month + VAT for up to 5 carers. From 6 carers, Pro is £100 per month + VAT (the first 10 licences are included), then £10 per carer + VAT. Telephone support and training is an optional add-on at £39 per month + VAT. The recruitment portal is an optional add-on at £49 per month + VAT. Your coordinators, managers and back-office team are always unmetered, and there is no cap on service users.

Home care software in the UK typically costs £15-30 per staff member per month, and most providers charge for every login, including office coordinators and managers, not just the carers doing visits. HomecareOS prices differently: it starts at £49 per month + VAT for up to 5 carers, then grows with your licences. Unlimited office staff and unlimited service users are included, so a 30-carer agency with 4 coordinators only pays for the carers, not a fee for every login. Annual billing saves a further 20%. Every plan includes CQC, CIW, Care Inspectorate, and RQIA compliance frameworks for all four UK nations, so agencies in Wales or Scotland aren't paying for an England-only tool that doesn't fit their regulator.

      Getting Going

      Support and training, and what costs extra

      Software you cannot get started with is not cheap at any price. Here is what comes with each plan, and what sits outside the licence so there are no surprises later.

      Included

      Support by email and chat, included

      Every agency gets support by email and chat, to help you get your carers and service users in, build your first week of rota, and put the app on a carer's phone.

      Optional add-on

      Telephone support and training, £39 per month + VAT

      Telephone support and live training for your office team and carers is an optional add-on at £39 per month + VAT. It is available again when you take on new coordinators or change how you work.

      Optional extra

      Recruitment portal

      The recruitment portal is an optional add-on at £49 per month + VAT on every plan, including Pro: applicant tracking from invite to hire, with documents, checks and onboarding on one pipeline. See the add-on.

      Onsite training

      Training delivered at your office, for agencies that would rather get everyone in a room. This sits outside the licence and is quoted before anything is booked, because the cost depends on where you are and how many people are being trained.

      Bringing your live data across

      Your current carers, service users, care plans and the running rota are included, and we do the work rather than handing you an importer and wishing you well.

      That is deliberate. Getting in should not have a toll on it, and a charge on the way in sits uncomfortably close to a charge on the way out.

      How a migration runs

      Bringing your history across

      Past service users, former staff and old visit records are a separate job, and this one is quoted rather than fixed, because the price is set by what your current provider will actually hand over.

      A clean export of historic records is quick work. A provider who will only give you PDFs, or who makes you open records one at a time on screen, is not. We look at a sample first at no charge and quote from what is really there.

      It is also optional. Your statutory retention duty can be met by keeping the old system's export, so it is worth deciding on rather than assuming.
