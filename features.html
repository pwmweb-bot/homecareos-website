Who This Is For

Home Care, Not Care Homes

homecareOS is built exclusively for UK domiciliary agencies. Here's what that means in practice.

Does homecareOS work for residential care homes or nursing homes?

No. homecareOS is built exclusively for domiciliary home care, UK agencies whose carers travel to service users in their own homes. For residential care homes or nursing homes, we'd recommend Person Centred Software or Nourish, both of which specialise in residential settings.

What's the difference between "home care" and "care homes"?

Home care (also called domiciliary care) is when carers visit service users in their own homes for short visits, typically 30 minutes to an hour at a time. A care home is a residential facility where people live full-time and receive 24-hour support. They're regulated under different frameworks and need different software. homecareOS is for the former.

Which CQC framework does homecareOS support?

homecareOS aligns with CQC's "Domiciliary care agency" regulated activity (and the equivalent frameworks at CIW in Wales, Care Inspectorate in Scotland, and RQIA in Northern Ireland). PIR auto-population, KLOEs and inspection prep are all targeted at domiciliary settings.
