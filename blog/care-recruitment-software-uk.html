Best Care Recruitment Software in the UK: A Fair Round-up

Care recruitment software ranges from a full applicant tracking system for a large care group to a simple apply portal for a small home care agency. The right one depends on how many people you hire, where your adverts go, and what happens after the offer.

By Philip Martin · Founder, HomecareOS · Published 13 November 2026

Searching for "care recruitment software" brings up a long list of tools that all promise to fill your rota. Some are full applicant tracking systems built for care groups with hundreds of vacancies. Some are HR platforms with hiring included. Some are recruitment modules inside a care management system. One of the best-known names in social care is not an applicant tracking system at all, but a staff referral app.

This round-up is written for UK home care agencies. It describes what each tool says it does on its own public pages, gives prices only where the supplier publishes them, and is upfront that HomecareOS is our product. Features and prices change, so check each supplier's current page before you decide.

If you are still working from spreadsheets, start with our guide to applicant tracking for home care agencies, which explains why a staged pipeline matters.

What care recruitment software has to do

Before comparing suppliers, be clear on the job. For a CQC-registered home care agency, recruitment software is not just about getting more applicants. It is about getting the right people through safer recruitment checks quickly, with evidence you can show an inspector.

CQC's guidance on Regulation 19: fit and proper persons employed expects providers to operate robust recruitment procedures and hold the information set out in Schedule 3 for each person employed. That includes proof of identity, a criminal record check, satisfactory evidence of conduct in previous care work, and a full employment history with written explanations of any gaps.

On top of that, every employer has to carry out right to work checks in the way the Home Office sets out in its employer's guide to right to work checks, and care roles normally need the right level of DBS check, which GOV.UK's DBS eligibility tool can help you confirm.

So at a minimum, good care recruitment software should help you to:

  • Collect complete applications, including full employment history and gap explanations.
  • Track each applicant through stages, so you can see who is waiting on references, DBS or an interview.
  • Hold the evidence in one record: right to work, DBS details, references and health information.
  • Keep applicants moving, with reminders and simple communication, because people who wait too long take another job.
  • Hand over cleanly once hired, without re-typing everything into another system.

Our care worker recruitment checklist and guide to safer recruitment in domiciliary care go through those checks in more detail.

Three types of care recruitment software

Most tools fall into one of three groups. Knowing which group you need narrows the list fast.

  1. Recruitment inside a care management system. Hiring sits in the same system as staff records and the rota. The main benefit is that a hired applicant becomes a staff or carer record without re-keying. HomecareOS, CarePoint365, Carionex and Lumio are examples.
  2. Care-focused HR and applicant tracking systems. Dedicated hiring or HR tools built for care providers, often with stronger job-board posting, screening and onboarding. CVMinder ATS, CarerHR and CareVeya sit here, and Jobtrain is a larger ATS with a dedicated care offer.
  3. Recruitment channels and add-ons. Tools that bring more applicants in but do not replace an ATS. Care Friends, a staff referral app, is the best-known example in social care.

The round-up

Listed by type, not ranked. Each summary is based on the supplier's public pages as checked in November 2026.

HomecareOS recruitment portal

Type: recruitment inside a home care management system. Published price: £49 a month plus VAT.

HomecareOS is our own care management system for UK home care agencies, so treat this entry with that in mind. Here is what the recruitment portal does today:

  • The agency invites an applicant by email. SMS invites are not available.
  • The applicant completes an online apply portal with their application, documents, right to work, two references, DBS details and a health questionnaire.
  • The agency tracks each applicant through seven stages: application, documents, references, DBS, interview, offer and onboarding.

Best for: small and growing home care agencies that want safer recruitment evidence and the rota in one system. If you need to post adverts to many job boards in one click, or you hire at the scale of a large care group, look at the dedicated ATS options below as well.

CarePoint365

Type: recruitment inside a care platform.

CarePoint365's domiciliary care recruitment page describes hiring software for home care that covers advertising vacancies, collecting applications and tracking DBS, right to work, references and sponsored worker status. It says checks are set per vacancy, so candidates cannot move to offer until mandatory items are complete, and that hired carers flow into its HR module ready for rostering. It also describes dashboards scoped by branch or region.

Best for: multi-branch providers, and agencies employing sponsored workers who want those checks tracked alongside everything else.

Carionex

Type: recruitment inside a care platform.

Carionex's care recruitment software page describes advertising roles, an applicant pipeline with configurable stages, digital collection of right to work, DBS and reference documents, and a handover in which a hired candidate becomes a staff profile without re-keying, ready to be scheduled into visits.

Best for: agencies that want recruitment and scheduling in one platform and prefer configurable pipeline stages.

CarerHR

Type: care-focused HR and hiring. Published price: from £299 a month on its homepage.

CarerHR presents itself as HR and hiring for UK domiciliary care agencies. Its homepage describes a branded careers site, references that are requested and chased automatically, a portal where each new starter uploads documents and completes checks, and expiry tracking with reminders. It says every plan includes recruitment, a document tracker, a staff portal, leave, e-sign and a CQC compliance manager, with the plan set by agency size.

Best for: agencies that want hiring and ongoing HR compliance together, and already have a separate system for rotas and care records.

CVMinder ATS

Type: care-focused applicant tracking system. Published price: from £79 plus VAT a month on its care page.

CVMinder ATS for care is an applicant tracking system for UK care providers recruiting for themselves. Its pages describe posting to free and paid job sites, flexible online application forms including work history and gap explanations, screening tools, built-in candidate communication, an employment reference audit trail and an auditable onboarding checklist.

Best for: agencies whose main problem is attracting and sorting applicants from job boards, and that want an affordable dedicated ATS.

CareVeya

Type: care-focused safer recruitment and workforce compliance.

CareVeya's safer recruitment page describes taking a carer from application to first shift, reading DBS certificate details from uploads, checking status on the DBS Update Service with consent, guiding share code right to work checks through GOV.UK, and holding visa hour limits on the rota. It is clear that it cannot confirm a share code itself, as only the Home Office can.

Best for: providers who want checks to block a shift until they are complete, including visa work-hour limits.

Jobtrain

Type: larger applicant tracking system with a care offer.

Jobtrain's applicant tracking system for care describes a branded careers site, free advertising channels including Indeed and Google for Jobs, a job board marketplace, video interviewing, bulk recruitment tools, self-service onboarding and compliance, and right to work and DBS checks run from inside the platform. It also integrates with Care Friends.

Best for: care groups with a dedicated recruitment team and high hiring volumes.

Care Friends

Type: staff referral app, not an ATS. Published price: £13.50 plus VAT per employee per month for organisations with up to 500 staff, with a minimum order and set-up fee.

Care Friends is an employee referral, recognition and reward app for social care. Staff share vacancies with friends and family from their phone and earn points as their candidates move through recruitment. It works alongside your recruitment process rather than replacing it.

Best for: agencies whose staff already recommend friends, and that want to reward referrals in stages rather than only after probation.

How to choose

Start from your own process, not a feature list. These questions usually settle it.

  • How many people do you hire a year? A small agency hiring a handful of carers a month rarely needs a large enterprise ATS. A care group running bulk recruitment days probably does.
  • Where do your applicants come from? If most come from job boards, posting and screening matter most. If most come from referrals and local word of mouth, a simple apply portal and a referral scheme may do more.
  • What happens after the offer? Count how many times a new carer's details are typed in between "yes" and their first visit. That is where recruitment and rota systems that talk to each other pay for themselves.
  • Can you show an inspector one person's full file in a minute? Ask each supplier to show you, in a demo, a single hired applicant's history, references, DBS details and right to work evidence.
  • Who does the checks? Find out whether the tool runs right to work and DBS checks through a certified provider, or helps you collect and record evidence while you do the check. You remain responsible either way.
  • What does it really cost? Ask about per-user or per-employee charges, set-up fees, job board costs and minimum terms. Only compare published prices like for like.

Whatever you choose, it will not fix slow decisions on its own. Applicants drop out when they wait. Our guides to onboarding new carers and carer turnover cover what happens after the software has done its part.

Frequently asked questions

What is care recruitment software?

Software that helps a care provider advertise roles, collect applications, track each applicant through stages such as interview, references and DBS, and keep the safer recruitment evidence in one record. Many tools call themselves an applicant tracking system, or ATS.

Do I need a care-specific applicant tracking system?

Not always, but a care-specific tool usually understands checks that general hiring software does not, such as employment history gaps, enhanced DBS, references from previous care work and right to work evidence. CQC Regulation 19 expects you to hold that information for every person you employ.

How much does care recruitment software cost?

It varies widely. Some suppliers publish prices, for example CVMinder ATS from £79 plus VAT a month on its care page, CarerHR from £299 a month, and the HomecareOS recruitment portal at £49 a month plus VAT. Many larger systems quote on request. Check each supplier's current pricing page before you decide.

Can recruitment software do right to work and DBS checks for me?

Some tools connect to certified identity service providers or the DBS, and others help you collect and record the evidence. Either way, the employer stays responsible for the check. GOV.UK sets out how right to work checks must be done, including the online check using a share code.

Should recruitment software connect to my rota system?

For a home care agency it helps a lot. If your recruitment tool and your care management system are separate, someone usually has to re-type each new carer's details and documents. Tools that hand a hired applicant straight into a staff or carer record save that step.
