Care Planning Software & Digital Care Records

Care planning software for UK domiciliary agencies, with person-centred digital care records and complete audit trails. Dynamic care plans, electronic MAR, body maps, observations and outcome-focused goals, all linked to every visit, accessible from anywhere.

Built for home care, not care homes. homecareOS care records are designed around domiciliary visits, short interactions in a service user's own home. If you run a residential care home or nursing home, Person Centred Software or Nourish have richer records for 24-hour residential settings.
DIGITAL CARE RECORDS

From Paper Mountains to Perfect Records

Filing cabinets overflowing with paper care plans. Illegible handwriting on MAR charts. Hours wasted searching for the right file before an inspection. Sound familiar? homecareOS replaces it all with structured, searchable, real-time digital records that carers actually want to use, and regulators love to see.

Why Digital Records Matter

Better Records, Better Care

Every feature is designed to improve outcomes for service users while making life easier for carers and managers.

Person-Centred Care Plans

Dynamic care plans that evolve with the service user. Linked to visits so carers always have the latest information at the point of care.

Electronic MAR (eMAR)

Medication administration records at point of care. Dosage prompts, refusal recording, and automatic alerts for missed medications.

Body Map Documentation

Visual body maps for recording skin integrity, bruising, and pressure areas. Photo evidence attached to specific body regions.

Outcome-Focused Goals

Set measurable goals for each service user and track progress over time. Evidence-based care that regulators want to see.

Immutable Audit Trail

Every entry is timestamped and attributed. Care notes and MAR records can never be modified or deleted, meeting the evidence requirements of CQC, CIW, the Care Inspectorate and RQIA.

Observation Tracking

Record vital signs, mood, fluid intake, nutrition, and wellbeing observations with trending over time.

Care Conversations

Turn a Conversation Into a Care Plan

Sit with the person and their family and simply talk, section by section. homecareOS records each part of the conversation, transcribes it, and drafts structured, person-centred care-plan content from what was actually said. You review and approve every section before anything reaches the plan, so the person's own words shape their care and your team keeps clinical judgement where it belongs. Available on the Pro plan.

Record the assessment section by section, as a natural conversation

Automatic transcription of what the person and their family actually said

AI drafts structured, person-centred care-plan content from the transcript

Review, edit and approve every section before it reaches the care plan

The person's own words shape the plan, not notes typed up hours later

Runs on the app for your carers and field assessors

Draft, transcript and approved version all kept for your audit trail

eMAR

A Week of Medication, One Clear Chart

The eMAR chart shows every scheduled medication round across the week at a glance. Given, refused and missed doses each carry their own code, so managers can spot a pattern before it becomes a problem, and every entry is timestamped and attributed for a complete audit trail.
