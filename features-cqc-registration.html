CQC REGISTRATION

Start Before You Are Registered

Most care software makes you wait until you are registered before you can even log in. homecareOS works the other way round. Sign up on the free tier while your application is still in progress, set your agency up properly, and use the registration checklist to keep every requirement in one place. The day your registration lands, you are not starting from scratch. You are ready to roster carers and deliver your first visit.

How It Works

From First Login to First Visit

Four steps that take you from a blank page to a registered, operating agency, without losing anything along the way.

1. Sign up free

Create your agency on the free starter tier with no software bill and no card. You can begin the moment you decide to set up, long before your registration is granted.

2. Work the checklist

Move through each registration requirement in order. The checklist tracks progress, holds your evidence, and shows you exactly what is left to do before you can submit.

3. Submit with confidence

Apply to your regulator with a complete, evidenced application. A tidy submission avoids the back and forth and delays that come from missing information.

4. Go live day one

When your registration is granted, your agency, your policies and your team are already in homecareOS. Build the rota, add service users, and deliver your first visit straight away.

The Checklist

Everything a New Provider Has to Get Right

The registration checklist covers the requirements a new domiciliary care provider is assessed on, so nothing is a surprise on submission.

Registered provider and registered manager applications

Track both applications side by side, with the fit and proper person evidence each one requires.

Statement of purpose

Set out who you are, the services you provide, your aims and the people you support, kept with the rest of your evidence.

Policies and procedures

Adopt a full set of regulator-aligned policies and procedures rather than writing them from a blank page.

DBS and background checks

Record DBS, PVG or AccessNI checks against each person, with the right check type for your nation and expiry reminders built in.

Recruitment and safer hiring

Applications, references and right to work in one place, so your recruitment records stand up to scrutiny from day one.

Training and the Care Certificate

Track mandatory training and Care Certificate progress for every new starter, with alerts before anything expires.

Insurance and financial viability

Keep your insurance and the evidence of financial viability that the regulator asks a new provider to demonstrate.

Ready to deliver safe care

Care planning, medication and safeguarding processes set up and evidenced, so you can show you are ready to operate safely, not just on paper.

Not Just England

Registering in Any UK Nation

The checklist follows your nation's regulator, so it is right whether you are registering in England, Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland.

England, CQC

Registered provider and manager, statement of purpose, DBS, and readiness for the single assessment framework.

Wales, CIW

Registration with Care Inspectorate Wales, DBS checks and Social Care Wales registration for your workforce.

Scotland, Care Inspectorate

Registration with the Care Inspectorate, PVG scheme membership and SSSC registration for staff.

Northern Ireland, RQIA

Registration with RQIA, AccessNI checks and NISCC registration for your care workers.
