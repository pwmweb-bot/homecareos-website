Policies & Procedures

40 UK Care Policy & Procedure Templates, Already Written

Most agencies inherit a Word folder of stale policies last touched in 2018. homecareOS replaces that mess with a living, breathing policy library — 40 policy and procedure templates designed in line with how the platform actually works, every document mapped to CQC, CIW, Care Inspectorate or RQIA frameworks, every revision tracked, every staff acknowledgement timestamped. When regulations change, we tell you which policies are affected and propose the new wording. You review, approve, publish — and the next inspector sees a tidy audit trail instead of a panic.
