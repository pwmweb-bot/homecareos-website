Enterprise Home Care Software With One Login for Every Branch

Running more than one branch usually means logging in and out all day and stitching the figures together in a spreadsheet on a Monday morning. homecareOS puts every branch you run behind one login, compares them all on the same measures, and drops you into any one of them from the keyboard.

ENTERPRISE AND MULTI-BRANCH

See every branch side by side, then drop into the one that needs you

The group dashboard lines up every branch you can reach on one screen: visits delivered, punctuality, continuity, medication, training, sickness and money. Each branch is marked against the group median, the best in the group stands out in green, and anything that needs a look stands out in red.

Above the table, a short list tells you where to look first: the branch whose continuity has slipped well below the rest, the branch delivering care with no medication recorded, the branch that leads the group on time. One press of Drop in and you are working inside that branch.

WHY IT MATTERS

Branch figures only mean something when every branch is counted the same way

A group director comparing branches from separate reports is usually comparing different sums. One branch counts cancelled visits, another counts next week's bookings, and a small branch with a bad week drags the group average as hard as a large one. homecareOS counts every branch with the same definitions, from the same records, at the same moment.

Weighted, not averaged

Group percentages are built from every branch's own numbers, so a thirty-visit branch cannot move the group figure as much as a three-thousand-visit one. Averaging branch percentages is one of the easiest ways for a group report to mislead the people reading it.

Only what has happened

A visit counts towards delivered once its window has closed. Future bookings never sit in a denominator waiting to make a branch look worse, and cancelled visits are left out altogether.

None recorded is not zero

A branch that has not recorded something yet says so, rather than showing a zero that reads like a failure or a perfect score. A real zero is still shown as a zero, because that one is worth a look.

CARE QUALITY

Compare care quality across every branch you run

Six measures of the care each branch is actually delivering, each defined the same way everywhere in homecareOS.

Visits delivered

Completed visits as a share of the visits that were due. Future bookings and cancellations are left out, so a branch is never marked down for visits that have not happened yet.

On time

Arrivals no more than 15 minutes after the planned start, measured on the lead carer's clock-in. Early arrivals count as on time.

Missed visits

Visits nobody clocked in to before the branch's missed-visit threshold. A useful early warning when a branch is short or its carers are not using clock-in.

Continuity of carer

For each person, the share of their visits carried out by the carer they see most, averaged across everyone the branch supports. The same measure as the Care continuity report.

Doses given

Of the regular medication entries recorded, the share marked given or self-administered. Refusals and stock shortages make up the rest.

Open incidents

Incidents still open, whenever they were raised, so nothing slips out of sight because it happened last month.

The dashboard tells you where to look first

Above the benchmark table, the group dashboard lists the few things worth your attention first. A branch is raised only when its figure is at least 10 points below the group median and there are at least 20 events behind it, so one bad day at a small branch never sends you chasing a false alarm. A branch clearly leading the group on time is called out too, so you can find out what its coordinators do differently.

WORKFORCE AND MONEY

Spot the branch that is short, untrained or waiting to be paid

The workforce and money tabs sit beside care quality, so a dip in punctuality can be read against how many carers are working, how much training has lapsed and what is still owed.

Carers working

Distinct carers who clocked in at least once in the period: the people actually out on visits, not everyone still on the books.

People supported

Active service users at each branch, so every other figure can be read against the size of the branch.

Training in date

Courses in date out of every course assigned to the branch's carers, using the same rule as the training matrix. Courses booked but not yet due are left out.

Bradford 200+

Carers whose Bradford Factor is 200 or more over the last 12 months, where short, repeated sickness spells weigh far more than one long absence.

Invoiced

Invoices issued in the period, by the date they were sent, the same date that reaches Xero or Sage.

Outstanding

The unpaid balance at the end of the period, after part payments and credit notes.

SWITCHING

Switch branch from the keyboard, not a new login

Cmd+B from anywhere

Press Cmd+B, or Ctrl+B on Windows, on any dashboard screen to open the branch switcher. Press the number beside a branch to drop straight into it. The shortcut steps aside while you are typing, so bold still works in your notes.

Your role travels with you

You can be a registered manager at one branch and read-only at another. Whichever branch you are in, you work with the role you hold there.

Nothing crosses between branches

When you switch, homecareOS clears what it was holding for the old branch and rebuilds every screen for the new one, so one branch's figures never appear under another's name.

Every switch is on the record

Each change of branch is written to the audit log of both the branch you left and the branch you moved into, with who switched and when.

ACCESS AND FOUR NATIONS

Everyone sees exactly the branches they have been given

Access by accepted invitation

Joining another branch is by invitation, and the person has to accept it before it gives them anything. Until then the branch does not appear in their switcher and cannot be opened.

Money only with finance access

Invoiced and outstanding figures appear only for branches where your role includes finance. Everyone else sees care and workforce figures without the money.

Nothing you could not already open

The group dashboard never shows a branch you could not open yourself. It is a faster way to see what you already have access to, never a way round it.

A group that crosses a border

Each branch keeps its own nation and regulator, chosen when you add it: CQC, CIW, the Care Inspectorate or RQIA. The dashboard shows every branch's regulator beside its figures.

ENTERPRISE

Enterprise home care software for groups with more than one branch

Enterprise home care software is the system a home care group runs every branch on: scheduling, care records, compliance, people and money in one place, with firm control over who can see and change what. Here is what homecareOS gives a group today.

Every branch behind one login

Office staff can hold access to several branches under one login. Whoever creates a branch has access straight away, and staff from another branch join once they accept your invitation. Press Ctrl+B (Cmd+B on a Mac) and the branch's number to switch. In each branch they work with the role they hold there, and its screens show only that branch's records.

Nine office roles

Owner, registered manager, care manager, coordinator, senior carer, finance, HR, office administrator and read only. Each role has its own set of permissions, which decides the sections in that person's menu, and on the team screen managers can only invite someone to, or move them into, a role below their own.

Support access on the record

When the homecareOS support team needs to open your account to help, they have to give a reason before they can, and each support session is recorded.

Row-level security on every application table

Every application table in the homecareOS database has row-level security switched on. Care records, rotas and invoices are filtered to the branch you are signed in to, so staff in one branch cannot open another branch's records. The group dashboard compares branches using totals only.

An audit log of office corrections

When the office corrects clock times, swaps a carer on the Visits screen, deletes a visit created in error, records a medication after the visit, amends a visit task or voids an invoice, the audit log records who did it, their role and when. Owners and registered managers can search and filter it, and export what is on screen as a CSV.

Data held in Ireland

Your care records and uploaded files are stored in Ireland on Amazon Web Services, through our database provider Supabase, and the application's server functions run in Dublin. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and the database is backed up every day.

Moving a group across. When you come from another system, our team migrates your records for you. Staff and client lists come across through our import tool, and care plans are loaded from your old system's records. What comes across depends on what that system lets you export and on what homecareOS can take in. Past medication charts, for example, stay in your old system.

Built-in reports. Reports across operations, care quality, people and money, each with a CSV export. Each report covers the branch you are working in, and the group dashboard is where you compare branches side by side.
