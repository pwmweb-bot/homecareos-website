Visa Sponsorship

Built for UK Home Care Sponsors

Sponsor-licence compliance is a part-time job most agencies do as a full-time crisis. Spreadsheets of CoS numbers. Visa expiry dates in someone's calendar. Right to Work copies in a folder no one's audited. UKVI reports filed late, or not at all. One unannounced compliance visit and the licence is gone — along with the agency's ability to hire from overseas for the next five years.

homecareOS solves this the way it solves everything: by making the compliance trail a side-effect of normal operations. When you onboard a sponsored carer, the visa data captures itself. When you run a rota, salary records flow into the audit. When a carer's BRP is six months from expiry, the registered manager hears about it. When the carer's address changes, the 10-day UKVI report is staged automatically. You stay licensed, you keep recruiting, you sleep at night.

The Six Things Your Sponsor Licence Demands — All Tracked

📊

Sponsor Licence Dashboard

One screen showing your sponsor rating (A or B), CoS allocation used vs remaining, sponsored-worker headcount, upcoming licence renewal date, and any open UKVI reporting actions. Refreshed live as you onboard, change roles, or end employment.

CoS Allocation Tracking

Track each Certificate of Sponsorship from request through assignment, visa application, grant, expiry and renewal. Every CoS is linked to the carer record, the role, the salary and the supporting documents — so a UKVI auditor sees the full chain in two clicks.

Right to Work Workflow

Capture share code, BRP or passport at onboarding. Re-check before visa expiry. Time-stamped, audit-trailed, and stored against the carer record with photo evidence. Designed against the Home Office RTW Code of Practice — every check produces a defensible statutory excuse.

Visa Expiry Alerts

Six-month, three-month, and 30-day warnings per carer. Surface to the registered manager and to the carer via the mobile app. Renewal application workflow keeps the chain of evidence in one place — no more "did anyone book the renewal?" panic at 60 days out.

📨

10-Day UKVI Reporting

UKVI requires sponsors to report changes within 10 working days. homecareOS detects the trigger events — address change, role change, salary change, employment ending, prolonged absence — and stages the report with the data you need to file via the Sponsorship Management System (SMS). Audit log records who did what, when.

📚

Sponsored-Carer Onboarding

Different documentation path than UK-resident carers: CoS reference, visa, BRP or eVisa share code, English language proof, accommodation arrangements, financial maintenance evidence. The onboarding flow knows the difference and asks for what's actually required for each route.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Health and Care Worker visa?

A sub-category of the UK Skilled Worker visa, opened to social care roles in February 2022. Allows UK home care agencies (and other regulated care providers) holding a sponsor licence to hire eligible workers from overseas to fill defined roles such as Senior Care Worker (SOC 6135) and Care Worker (SOC 6136).

What is a sponsor licence?

Permission from UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI, part of the Home Office) for an employer to sponsor migrant workers under the points-based immigration system. Home care agencies that want to recruit overseas carers under the Health and Care Worker visa must hold a Worker sponsor licence.

What is a Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS)?

A digital reference number issued from your sponsor licence that you assign to a specific worker for a specific role. The worker uses the CoS to apply for their visa. Defined CoS are the type used for Skilled Worker visas, including Health and Care Worker.

What are the main sponsor licence duties?

Five duties matter most: (1) Right to Work checks before employment starts and at visa expiry, (2) keeping records of sponsored workers — passport, visa, BRP/share code, contact details, attendance, (3) reporting changes to UKVI within 10 working days (address change, role change, salary change, employment ending, absences), (4) maintaining genuine vacancies (no over-sponsoring), and (5) cooperating with Home Office compliance audits.

What happens if you lose your sponsor licence?

All sponsored workers become unable to continue working for you and typically have 60 days to find a new sponsor or leave the UK. The agency itself faces a 5-year cooling-off period before being allowed to apply for a new licence. Several UK home care agencies have lost licences during the 2024-2025 compliance crackdown — most commonly for inadequate record-keeping or late UKVI reports.

Does homecareOS report directly to UKVI on my behalf?

No — the legal duty to report sits with the licence holder, and reporting is done through the Sponsorship Management System (SMS) by your authorising officer or Level 1 user. What homecareOS does is surface the events that trigger a 10-day report and store the supporting evidence in audit-ready format.

What is the salary threshold for the Health and Care Worker visa?

As of April 2024 the minimum gross salary is the higher of £23,200 per year or the going rate for the role. Senior Care Worker (SOC 6135) and Care Worker (SOC 6136) have specific going rates set by occupation. The threshold has been rising and is expected to continue increasing — sponsors must check current values on gov.uk before issuing a CoS.
