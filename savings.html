How we estimated yearly savings

This page is the basis for the claim that estimated yearly savings can run into hundreds for a start-up, thousands for a small agency, and up to tens of thousands for a medium agency, against buying care software, recruitment and a phone system separately. These are estimates, and your figures will vary.

The comparison was prepared on 20 November 2026. Competitor prices are published list prices on that date. They change, and many agencies pay a negotiated rate.

The headline figures

The figures below are estimated yearly savings on monthly billing, using CareLineLive’s published Bring Your Own Device price as the care-software comparison. A range against The Access Group’s published home care software prices, and the same model on annual HomecareOS billing (20% off the plan), is in the full table later on this page.

Estimated yearly saving, CareLineLive base case, monthly billing, 20 November 2026
Agency size Carers in the model Separate stack, per month HomecareOS, per month Estimated yearly saving
Start-up 5 £239 £156 £996
Small 20 £549 £341.50 £2,490
Medium (lower) 60 £1,616 £783 £9,996
Medium 100 £2,450 £1,244 £14,472

That is why we say hundreds for a start-up, thousands for a small agency, and up to tens of thousands for a medium agency. The medium figure passes £20,000 a year only at around 100 carers, and only against the higher end of The Access Group’s published range (see the sensitivity columns below). Against CareLineLive at 100 carers the base case is £14,472 a year.

Agency website hosting is listed as Coming soon on our pricing page, with no public price, so it has been left out of both sides of this comparison. The original working model also included a separately bought agency website. We excluded that line and recomputed the yearly savings, which are £348 a year lower than the version that included a website. The phone system is available to set up in HomecareOS today; call recording remains Coming soon and is not part of this comparison. The minute and text tariffs used below are the current in-app rates, which are not yet itemised on the public pricing page.

HomecareOS prices used

HomecareOS public prices, as set out on our pricing page:

  • Start is £49 per month for up to 5 carers.
  • Pro is £100 per month for up to 10 carers, then £10 per extra carer.
  • Annual billing is 20% off the plan. Office users and service users are unmetered.
  • Telephone support and training is an optional add-on at £39 per month. It is included on the HomecareOS side of this comparison so that support is treated consistently with CareLineLive, whose published price includes phone support.
  • The recruitment portal is an optional add-on at £49 per month, and is included where the separate stack also includes recruitment software.

Phone and texts, using current in-app tariffs (not yet itemised on the public pricing page):

  • The Start plan does not include the phone system, so the start-up example keeps the CircleLoop phone and text prices on both sides.
  • Pro includes the phone system, with an in-app allowance of 200 bundle minutes and 100 texts. Extra minutes are bought in bundles: 600 minutes for £25, 300 for £13, and 100 for £5. Extra texts are 7p each.
  • Call recording is Coming soon and is not included in the HomecareOS figures.
  • The public pricing page still labels the phone system Coming soon as of 20 November 2026. The system itself can be set up now.

Against The Access Group at £25 per carer, with HomecareOS on annual billing, the 100-carer example saves £23,232 a year. That is the top of the range in this model, and the reason the wording is “up to tens of thousands” rather than a flat “tens of thousands”.

Where the money comes from:

  • Most of the saving is the per-carer care software price. At 100 carers, CareLineLive at £19.70 per carer is £1,970 a month, against a HomecareOS Pro plan of £1,000 a month.
  • The next largest item is recruitment software: £350 a month against the HomecareOS recruitment portal at £49 a month, for a medium agency.
  • Phone and texts are roughly a wash, and can cost slightly more on HomecareOS for heavier callers, because texts are 7p rather than 4p and minutes are metered, whereas CircleLoop’s published price includes unlimited UK calls. That is already reflected in the figures above.

What we have not counted

  • Agency website hosting. This is listed as Coming soon on our pricing page, with no public price, so it is excluded from both sides. A separately bought website would add to the cost of the separate stack, but we have not put that into the published figures.
  • One-off onboarding or implementation fees. Some platforms charge to get you started. Our migration page notes, for example, Access implementation fees on their G-Cloud listing from £720 to several thousand, and a typical everyLIFE one-off fee of £3,600 for implementation and training. Those amounts are not in the yearly tables. Avoiding them would increase the saving in the first year.
  • Social posts and design retainers, paid job ads, and SEO retainers. HomecareOS does not replace paid job ads or an SEO retainer, so they are left out of both sides. Social posting is also left out.
  • Printing of paper care plans, MAR charts and rotas. We did not find a reliable sourced figure, so this is upside only and is not in the tables.
  • A start-up already on another platform’s starter tier for very small agencies would save little or nothing on care software. The start-up figures above assume the agency would otherwise pay a typical published list price, such as CareLineLive’s £120 monthly minimum.

These are estimates, and your figures will vary

Please treat this page as a worked comparison, not a quote. In particular:

  • List prices change, and many agencies pay a negotiated rate rather than the published price.
  • Your carer count, office-user count, call minutes and text volumes will not match the worked examples exactly. Minutes and texts in the model are our estimates.
  • The phone tariffs on the HomecareOS side are current in-app rates and are not yet itemised on the public pricing page.
  • If you do not need a recruitment tool, the saving is smaller, as the “no recruitment tool” start-up row shows.
  • The comparison is dated 20 November 2026. If HomecareOS or competitor prices change, these figures need to be recalculated.

Sources

  • CareLineLive pricing, read 20 November 2026, for the Bring Your Own Device price of £19.70 per carer per month and the £120 per month minimum, and for phone support being included in that price.

  • Harbour pricing and packages, read 20 November 2026, for SRS Lite at £100 per month (under 50 employees) and Recruit from £350 per month (50 or more).
  • CircleLoop pricing, read 20 November 2026, for £15 per user per month, unlimited UK calls, and texts at 4p.
  • HomecareOS pricing, for Start, Pro, annual billing, telephone support and the recruitment portal.
  • HomecareOS switching and migration, for the one-off implementation fees we have not counted in the yearly tables.
